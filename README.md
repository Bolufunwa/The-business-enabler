# The Business Enabler – GRC (Governance, Risk and Compliance) Framework for Nexus Financial Services

This project presents a foundational Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) framework for Nexus Financial Services, a growing financial services company based in Lagos, Nigeria, with approximately $800 million in assets. It identifies key governance, risk management, and regulatory compliance gaps and provides practical recommendations to strengthen accountability, protect organizational assets, and support sustainable business growth.

## Project Overview

The purpose of this project is to assess the GRC challenges facing Nexus Financial Services and recommend a structured, risk-based framework to improve its organizational governance, risk management, and regulatory compliance.

The assessment focuses on three core GRC pillars:

- **Governance:** Establishing clear policies, decision-making structures, approval processes, accountability, and escalation procedures.
- **Risk Management:** Identifying, assessing, and mitigating internal, external, financial, operational, and cybersecurity risks.
- **Compliance:** Ensuring adherence to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

The project identifies three major organizational gaps:

1. Lack of a clear process for approving and enforcing company policies.
2. Absence of a standardized risk assessment process before business decisions are made.
3. Lack of assigned responsibility for tracking regulatory requirements and compliance deadlines.

## Network Topology

This project focuses on developing an organizational GRC framework rather than designing or configuring a computer network.

The assessment considers the company's organizational governance structures, risk management processes, internal controls, and regulatory compliance responsibilities.

## Tools and Technologies

The project focuses on GRC frameworks, regulatory requirements, and risk management concepts.

- **Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Framework:** A structured approach to integrating governance, risk management, and regulatory compliance.
- **Risk Assessment:** Identification and evaluation of organizational risks based on their likelihood and potential impact.
- **Risk and Control Register:** A centralized document for recording identified risks, associated controls, and regulatory requirements.
- **Internal Controls:** Preventive and detective measures designed to reduce organizational risk exposure.
- **Regulatory Compliance References:**
  - Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulations and directives.
  - Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act, 2022.
  - Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023.
- **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC):** Regulatory body responsible for overseeing applicable personal data protection obligations.

## Configuration Steps

1. **Conduct a Baseline GRC Assessment**

   - Review existing governance structures, risk management practices, and regulatory compliance procedures.
   - Identify weaknesses and gaps across the three GRC pillars.

2. **Identify Governance Gaps**

   - Assess existing policy approval and enforcement processes.
   - Identify unclear decision-making authority and accountability.
   - Recommend appropriate approval requirements and escalation procedures.

3. **Assess Organizational Risks**

   - Identify potential risks relating to fraud, data privacy, cybersecurity, and inadequate internal controls.
   - Evaluate identified risks based on their likelihood and potential impact.
   - Recommend preventive and detective controls to mitigate identified risks.

4. **Identify Regulatory Compliance Requirements**

   - Identify applicable Nigerian legal and regulatory requirements.
   - Review relevant CBN regulations and directives.
   - Consider the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act, 2022, and Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023.
   - Assign responsibility for monitoring regulatory changes and compliance deadlines.
   - Verify applicable obligations based on the company's license, business activities, and operations.

5. **Develop a Centralized Risk and Control Register**

   - Document key organizational risks.
   - Record relevant controls and regulatory requirements.
   - Establish a centralized approach to monitoring identified risks and control weaknesses.

6. **Implement the GRC Framework in Phases**

   - Prioritize operational risks and regulatory compliance.
   - Gradually expand the framework into a more comprehensive GRC programme.

7. **Establish Continuous Monitoring and Improvement**

   - Conduct regular management reviews.
   - Perform control testing to evaluate control effectiveness.
   - Monitor progress and ensure timely remediation of identified issues.

## Results and Findings

The assessment identified significant gaps in Nexus Financial Services' existing GRC structure and established recommendations for addressing them.

**Key Findings:**

- **Governance:** The absence of formal policy approval processes and clearly defined responsibilities creates accountability and decision-making challenges.
- **Risk Management:** The lack of a standardized risk assessment process increases exposure to fraud, cybersecurity threats, data privacy issues, and inadequate internal controls.
- **Compliance:** The absence of an assigned compliance monitoring responsibility creates the possibility of missed regulatory requirements and deadlines.

**Expected Outcomes:**

The proposed foundational, risk-based GRC framework is expected to deliver the following outcomes:

- Improved organizational accountability through clearly defined roles and responsibilities.
- Stronger regulatory compliance through the identification and monitoring of applicable legal requirements.
- Reduced risk exposure through structured risk assessments and appropriate internal controls.
- Improved visibility of organizational risks through a centralized risk and control register.
- Timely identification and remediation of control weaknesses.
- Enhanced operational resilience and business continuity.
- Protection of company assets, customer information, and organizational reputation.
- A stronger foundation for sustainable business growth.
